Vision

To raise a generation of young people who know God, discover their purpose, develop their talents, and become lights that transform communities, nations and the world.

Mission

To empower young people spiritually, socially, educationally, and economically through mentorship, discipleship, education, leadership development, skills training and community outreach, helping them become responsible leaders and agents of positive change.

Core targets for young people

Spiritual growth

  • Help young people build a strong relationship with God.
  • Teach biblical values, integrity and godly character.
  • Raise young leaders who influence others positively.

Education and knowledge

  • Support access to quality education.
  • Encourage reading, learning and discovering purpose.
  • Offer mentorship and career guidance.

Leadership development

  • Train young people to become responsible leaders.
  • Build confidence, discipline and teamwork.
  • Develop future community and national leaders.

Skills and empowerment

  • Provide practical skills training.
  • Encourage entrepreneurship and innovation.
  • Create opportunities for self-reliance and economic growth.

Community impact

  • Promote acts of love, service and compassion.
  • Support vulnerable children, families and communities.
  • Inspire young people to become problem solvers and peacemakers.

Long-term goals

  • Establish youth mentorship programs.
  • Develop educational support initiatives.
  • Create skills training and empowerment centers.
  • Organize conferences, seminars and outreach programs.
  • Raise a generation of purpose-driven and Christ-centered leaders.
